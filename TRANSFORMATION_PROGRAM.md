# THE INNERVERSE PROGRAM — Transformation Program Design

**A 16-week guided parts-integration program, instrumented by the Innerverse app.**
Designed with the ACERI cycle (Attune → Co-create → Enact → Reflect → Integrate). All decisions are made and hardcoded; a facilitator delivers, the app measures, the human transforms.

**Evidence tags** (F3, applied to every claim): `[E]` established (replicated, mainstream) · `[H]` house-method (our synthesis, unproven) · `[C]` contested-theory (clinically used, not settled). Nothing untagged. No fabricated citations.

**Scope boundary (non-negotiable):** This is a psychoeducational transformation *program*, not therapy. It does not diagnose or treat mental illness. Strong-attractor / trauma / exile work is therapist-gated (mirrors app §3B RED tier). Clinical presentations are referred out, warmly. See §9.

---

## PART 1 — ATTUNE (the diagnosis, decided)

The learner supplied a written brief (5 documents). Per Brief Intake (F4), the six layers are extracted and **stated as decided diagnosis** — correct on contact, not interrogated.

### The six layers

1. **Struggling moment.** Not "I want to understand myself." The trigger is a *recurring behavioral collapse under load*: the person who reads every self-help book and still explodes at their partner, still numbs at 11pm, still shrinks in the meeting. The crack is the gap between insight and behavior — the exact gap "The Pattern"/"Co-Star" widen by selling more insight. `[H]`
2. **Full learning job (7 dims).** Functional: run their own inner system under stress. Emotional: move from self-contempt to self-leadership. **Social** (most-skipped): be experienced by intimates as less reactive, more present. **Anticipatory** (2nd most-skipped): feel the program was built for *their* specific parts, not a generic audience. Financial: fewer relationships/jobs burned by the pattern. Relational: not be alone in it. Spiritual: "my parts protected me; I can lead them." `[H]`
3. **Consequences (Level 3).** Level 1: conflicts, avoidance, lost sleep. Level 2: churned relationships, stalled career, cost of therapy-hopping. **Level 3 (existential):** "When I'm in the pattern, I become someone my kid is learning to be. Continuing this says I'm my wound, not my Self." This is where motivation actually lives. `[E]` (stakes must reach identity to drive behavior change).
4. **What failed before, and why.** Insight-only content (books, astrology apps) → **Degraded** failure mode: understood, couldn't apply to their own mess. Willpower → relapse. **Hidden benefit** (the skipped diagnostic): the problem identity ("I'm just an anxious person") excuses risk and lowers expectations — it must be named or the program yields completion without change. `[E]` Internalized voice: a shaming teacher/parent → activates the moment learning gets hard. Design must hold this, not ignore it. `[C]`
5. **Ecology.** The pattern is reinforced daily by partners, feeds, and frictionless AI companions that never push back (AGT reward-ecology). Skill trained in isolation dies in ~weeks against this. The program must engage the ecology (witnesses, environment cues), not just the learner. `[E]`
6. **Observable outcomes (P-Type, engineered).** Ranked by hurt:
   - **P1** — Minimize the **latency** from a felt trigger to an **unblended response** (naming the part instead of becoming it) in a real conflict.
   - **P2** — Minimize the **duration of secrecy** after a lapse (relapse/blow-up/shutdown) before disclosure to one human.
   - **P3** — Maximize the **rate at which a written if-then plan actually fires** under real load.
   - **P4** — Minimize the **time-to-repair** after interpersonal harm.
   D-Types (constellation beauty, belonging, awe) layer on top — never before P1–P4. `[E]`

### Learning Job Statement (the north star for every design decision)

> "When a familiar trigger hits under load, this person needs to **notice which part took the wheel, stay Self-led long enough to choose one controllable move, and repair fast when they miss** — so they can be the person their intimates and their own future self recognize — **without** collapsing into shame or licensing the behavior as 'just who I am.'"

If a feature doesn't serve this sentence, it's cut.

### Root-cause depth (CFM), attractor strength, arcs

- **Depth = L5 (Rules/Identity)** primary — "I *am* my wound" is a principle generating behavior across many situations — with **L3 (Structure/ecology)** and **L1 (State/regulation)** as mandatory prerequisites. An L4 reframe alone → "new story, no new behavior" (the exact failure mode we're built to beat). `[E]` Depth-match gate: every block below is checked to operate at L5 with L1/L3 scaffolding, never an L1 tweak sold as the L5 fix.
- **Attractor strength = STRONG** (childhood-origin, multi-domain, continuous activation, ecology-reinforced). Mandates: extensive early safety (Covenant, parts invitation, early wins), gradual challenge, relational disconfirmation across time, ecological embedding. Minimum viable *architecture* (not content) = a multi-month spiral. `[E]`
- **Arcs (mixed, named explicitly per phase):**
  - **Transformative** `[E]` — the core: activate the old protector prediction → experiential disconfirmation → felt shift → new encoding (memory reconsolidation).
  - **Amplification** `[E]` — Self-energy and resource parts are *already whole*; remove the internal cap, don't deficit-frame. Every phase opens strength-first (mirrors app "lead with the resource").
  - **Protection** `[C]` — for parts guarding boundary violation (Rebel/Warrior): channel healthy aggression into boundaries; do **not** try to "calm" it.
  - **Mourning** `[C]` — exile work (therapist-gated) often needs grief for what the wound cost; do not try to "complete/fix" irreparable loss.

**Cynefin:** the person's inner system is **Complex** (patterns hidden, outcome unpredictable) → East+South design (safe-to-fail probes + safety-first), not a North "just follow the steps" course. `[C]`

## PART 2 — CO-CREATE (direction chosen, trade-offs named)

Three directions were considered; **Direction B is selected and hardcoded.**

| Dir | Compass | Essence | Trade-off | Rejected because |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | North | Curriculum of IFS knowledge, module by module | High completion, low transfer | Reproduces the insight-without-change failure |
| **B (chosen)** | **West+South, East core** | **Instrumented practice loop: app measures real behavior between weekly sessions; sessions do reconsolidation on live material** | Requires the app + facilitator + adherence | Highest transfer; matches strong-attractor + L5 diagnosis |
| C | East | Pure emergent group process, no structure | Deep but unreproducible, unsafe for strong attractors | Fails safety sequencing for trauma-adjacent material |

**Authenticity test passed:** the facilitator cannot predict each learner's constellation, loops, or which disconfirmations land — participation is genuine co-creation, not scripted. **Shu/Ha/Ri:** program moves the learner Shu (guided) → Ha (adapts their own practices) → Ri (self-guides; graduation). `[H]`

**Five Field Forces audit** of Direction B: *Invitation* — the constellation is intrinsic pull. *Permission* — Covenant + parts-invitation before any challenge. *Affordance* — every gap ships a concrete app handle (a tap, an if-then, a check-in). *Amplification* — contributions change the visible constellation immediately. *Materiality* — the app IS a co-participant; small-group circles seated, not rows. All five present. `[H]`

## PART 3 — MACRO-ARC (Spiral Curriculum, 16 weeks, maps to app Practice Path §3D.3)

Culminates in **Resonance, not Tension** `[E]`. Engine order enforced (E1→E2→E3→E4) — firing out of order harms. `[C]/[E]`

| Phase | Wks | Engine | App chapter | Function | Must contain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I Stabilization** | 1–3 | E1 | Ch.1 Grounding | Settle the container | Learning Covenant, Proactive Parts Invitation, Quick Scan + Descent I, regulation skills, **guaranteed early win in first 7 days** |
| **II Differentiation** | 4–6 | E2 | Ch.2 Meeting | Observer online | Full assessment (Descents II–III), constellation mapping, unblending, "a part of me…" — the learner watches parts without being them |
| **III Processing** | 7–12 | E3 | Ch.3 Tending | Challenge + reconsolidate | ONE loop/complex per session, designed prediction error, polarization two-chair, firefighter urge work; internal CFM staircase (state→behavior→skill→meaning→identity) |
| **IV Integration** | 13–15 | E4 | Ch.4 Relating | Consolidate + transmit | Future-Self authoring, dual-bound charter, needs-request scripts, synastry (partner optional) |
| **V Graduation** | 16 | E4+E1 | — | Independence | Self-guided practice protocol, termination ritual, fade guidance, propagation invite |

**Total 16 wks** (strong attractor → extended Phase I before any E3). Weekly **Dual-Phase rhythm** `[E]`: Resonance session (circle/belonging) → Tension session (deep processing) → mixed (skill-building). Never deep E3 every week.

**Arrival-Fallacy inoculation** planted in Wk 2, fires Wks 6–12 (the standard dropout window): "The energy will fade and you'll think it's not working — that flat feeling is the new normal settling in, not failure." `[E]`

## PART 4 — THE BLOCKS (designed; facilitator delivers one Matryoshka element per turn, F6)

Spine shown. Each is Concept→Instruction→Experience→Sharing→Reflection; deep-change blocks use the 8-phase Extended cycle with Covenant+Yield. Beads: Reflection of block N = Concept of N+1.

### Block 1.1 — The Council Covenant (Phase I opener, Resonance-first)
- **Concept:** "You have spent years fighting your own reactions. What if none of them is the enemy?" Opens the gestalt closed in the Anchor.
- **Instruction (sensation→affect→image→concept):** feel where you brace when criticized → name its tone → picture it as a councillor at a table → *that's a part, and it once kept you safe*. `[C]` IFS "no bad parts."
- **Experience:** app Quick Scan → meet one part's card. Real material, immediately.
- **Sharing (process, not conclusions):** "What surprised you about meeting it?" + designed amplification: facilitator names the cross-room pattern.
- **Reflection (premise-level):** "What did you assume a 'bad habit' was, that you no longer assume?" + **WOOP** with named internal obstacle. **Identity anchor:** "I am someone who can meet a part instead of becoming it."
- **Covenant script** (mandatory, witnessed): "This will feel uncertain; that's the signal, not the problem. You're not alone in it — I'm here, and the observer part of you witnesses with care. Step back anytime by [signal]." `[C]` prosodic safety.

### Block 2.3 — Unblending under the Observer (E2 peak)
Target P1. Learner practices, in a *recalled* mild trigger, the shift "I am angry" → "a part of me is angry." Double-frame protects identity from in-role failure. Affordance: the app's 3-word part check-in. Measures act→essence latency as baseline for the OOD battery. `[H]`

### Block 3.x — Designed Prediction Error (E3 core, Transformative arc) — the engine
**Engine Readiness gate:** E1 (regulation) + E2 (observer) verified online first, or perturbation retraumatizes. `[E]`
- **Covenant + Yield** before perturbation.
- **Perturb:** elicit the protector's *specific* prediction verbatim ("If I set this boundary, I'll be abandoned"). Activate the old prediction — feel it, don't discuss it. `[E]` reconsolidation needs activation + *experienced* mismatch within the labile window `[E]`.
- **Regulate → Model → Activate:** run the smallest safe real experiment (relational/actional/imaginal disconfirmation).
- **Feel (Aesthetic of Holding):** "Before we analyze — what did that cost you?" Watch for the felt shift (sigh, softening, "it feels different now"). Slow down; let it settle.
- **Integrate (local, Bayesian — NOT a new global story):** "With this person, in this context, disclosure did not produce abandonment." Reject "this proves I'm worthy" (a global verdict is still a verdict — AGT). `[H]`
- **Bridge + Anchor:** if-then plan attached to the trigger cue; one-line note to former self.

### Block 4.2 — Future-Self & Dual-Bound Charter (E4, Amplification)
Author the Future-Self North Star (app §3D.2). Co-write the 6-sentence charter: upper bound (anti-shame) + lower bound (anti-license). Measure BOTH shame-drop and license-rise after — if compassion lowers shame but raises license, the block is miscalibrated, not done. `[H]` (from AGT dual-bound; the self-compassion→controllability mechanism is `[C]` *requires author verification*).

### Block 5.1 — Graduation (Resonance culmination)
Hand over the self-guided protocol: "The facilitator's voice becomes your own." Termination ritual (sensory anchor). Design for **obsolescence** — success is measured by learners who no longer need us, not re-enrollment. `[E]` Propagation invite: trained graduates can facilitate peer circles (the ultimate fractal — apply to others the arc applied to you).

## PART 5 — MEASUREMENT (F5: ≥2 outcome hooks per deliverable; hard-to-fake)

The program's differentiator and its honesty mechanism. Self-report is distrusted without behavioral probes (AGT). `[H]`

### Resistant outcome set (behavioral, from app event stream — never from "I feel better")
- **P1 act→essence latency** (OOD conflict probe, monthly)
- **P2 secrecy duration** (lapse log → "told a human" delta)
- **P3 if-then fire rate** (plans that fired under load / plans written)
- **P4 time-to-repair** (harm recognized → repair initiated)
- **Human-vs-app disclosure ratio** (re-embedding in real relationships)

**Clinical decision rule shown to facilitators verbatim:** "If weekly self-report improves but the resistant set does not, the learner is narrating change, not making it — stop adding insight, shift to skills/environment/exposure." `[H]`

### Spaced retrieval probes (R2 — dated, channel-named, never auto-implied)
Per key block: **Day 1** (2-min no-notes recall) → **Day 3–4** (apply to a new scenario) → **Wk 1–2** (interleave with next block's concept) → **Wk 4** (self-initiated). Delivered via app push (named channel); if push absent, shipped as text with the degraded-mode caveat. `[E]` testing effect + spacing.

### Four Testing Battery (which "learned" actually means, per outcome) `[E]`
Feynman (explain it) → Prediction (novel scenario) → **Boundary** (name 2 conditions where the move backfires) → **Transfer** (apply to an untaught domain). Program assesses to Prediction minimum; Transfer is the graduation bar. Feynman-only = informed, not transformed.

### Alignment-gap watch
Weekly self-report vs. behavioral/informant composite. A *growing* gap = the learner is getting fluent in therapy-talk while behavior stalls (narrative reward-hacking toward the facilitator) → hard pivot to environment/skills. `[H]`

## PART 6 — TRIPLE-HELIX & ECOLOGY (transfer design)

- **Triple-Helix** every block `[H]`: Narrative (learner is protagonist chairing a council), Cognitive (disequilibrium→construction→resolution explicit), Psychosomatic (body zone tapped, felt shift tracked). Cognitive-only = understands, doesn't change.
- **Distributed Redundancy** `[E]`: each new pattern practiced in 2–3 real contexts before next session; a named witness reinforces it; an environment cue prompts it. Strong attractors require engaging the *ecology*, not just the learner.
- **Witness (one human)** chosen Wk 1 (app §3C.4). The program structurally forbids the facilitator becoming the sole mirror — distributed witnessing enforced (anti-sycophancy). `[H]`

## PART 7 — FACILITATOR OPERATING SYSTEM (anti-drift)

The facilitator is an agent under the reward of being the idealized mirror. `[H]`
- **Post-session axis check** (2 min): Whose agency did I center? Did I accept a rescuer/sole-confidant role? Did I over-certain about "the real story"? Did I provide real friction or collude to avoid rupture? Was I flattered into it?
- **Calibrated dissent ladder** replaces auto-validation: reflect affect → separate suffering from conclusion → offer alternative inferences as hypotheses → stress-test against consequences/other minds → escalate to structured support if belief turns exclusive/paranoid/grandiose/self-harm-organizing.
- **Absolute prohibition:** facilitator is never the sole witness-authority of the learner's identity.

## PART 8 — REFLECT (double-loop + pre-mortem)

**Pre-mortem — it's 6 months out and the program failed. Five reasons + mitigations:**
1. *Insight theatre* — learners get fluent, don't change → **resistant outcome set + alignment-gap pivot rule** (Part 5).
2. *Trauma flooding* — E3 fired before E1/E2 → **Engine Readiness gate + exile work therapist-gated** (Parts 3–4, §9).
3. *Dropout at Wk 6–12* — energy dip → **Arrival-Fallacy inoculation + guaranteed Day-7 win** (Part 3).
4. *Ecology snapback* — skill dies at home → **Distributed Redundancy + named witness + environment cues** (Part 6).
5. *Facilitator becomes the crutch* — no graduation → **designed obsolescence + fading guidance + propagation** (Parts 4–7).

**Double-loop question standing for the design team:** "If we're wrong about anything, it's probably that ___" — hold this open; the app's telemetry (norm updates, adherence) is the falsifier.

## PART 9 — ETHICS, BOUNDARIES, DEGRADED MODES

- **Refuse cleanly, redirect warmly:** clinical diagnosis/treatment → "That's clinical territory; here is a referral process." Manipulative retention mechanics → build ethical engagement instead. Exploiting vulnerability → refuse. `[method ethics]`
- **Strong-attractor gate:** exile/reconsolidation of trauma is **therapist-gated** (app RED tier §3B). The program's solo track never walks an unsupervised learner into an exile. Crisis content → resources + human escalation.
- **No global verdicts, positive included** ("you are worthy" is banned exactly like "you are broken"). Every insight ends in a behavioral hook.
- **Environment Gate (degraded modes):** if no scheduler → spaced probes shipped as dated text, never a faked "I'll check in." If runtime can't deliver configured language → mirror the learner's language, drop persona, say so once. If the app isn't installed → the program degrades to worksheet + facilitator, explicitly weaker on P1–P4 measurement (state the degradation, don't hide it).

## PART 9B — EXPERIENTIAL DEPTH UPGRADES (Coherence Therapy + DNMS)

Two clinical sources sharpen the *mechanism* of the deep blocks. Additive, safety-preserving.

- **Coherence-first before disconfirmation (Block 3.x upgrade).** Insert a **Coherence beat** into every Perturb phase: surface *why the protector's prediction made sense* (its pro-symptom logic) **before** the mismatch. Reconsolidation requires the schema activated *with its coherence*, then juxtaposed. Skipping this yields a verbal reframe that doesn't take — the precise failure the program exists to beat. The deep-block template becomes the **4 C's** (Contact → Clarity → Coherence → Completion) layered over the engine sequence. `[C]`
- **Pendulation as the safety spine (all E3 work).** Never take the learner deeper into pain than resource is online; alternate resource↔need beats (somatic pendulation). Depth is bounded by the learner's resourced state — the facilitator's live version of the app's depth-gate (§3E.2). This *is* earned security. `[C]`
- **DNMS stabilization = Phase I hard prerequisites.** Before any E3: (1) mobilize the **Resource Team** by healing function (Nurturing/Protective/Wise-Core/Vital), (2) build a **Special Safe Place** for parts to rest between sessions, (3) set a goal wounded parts won't sabotage. No processing while any part objects (System-Wide Announcement). This makes the strong-attractor early-safety mandate concrete. `[C]`
- **The Harmless Recording (teach in Phase II).** "You're not reliving the event — you're reliving your *reaction* to a harmless recording." A precise unblending the learner carries into daily check-ins (app §3E.4). `[C]`
- **Metaprocessing (add to every E3/E4 debrief).** After the felt shift, *process the shift itself*: "What was it like to feel that change? What's newly possible?" (AEDP). Deepens encoding; the program currently under-metabolizes wins. `[C]`
- **Boundary:** EMDR/bilateral stimulation is clinician-only, contraindication-screened — **not** a program or app self-serve technique. Named here only to explicitly exclude it. `[method ethics]`

## PART 9C — SELF-LEADERSHIP UPGRADES (Talk Authentic IFS intensive)

Proven facilitation practices from the program owner's own intensive — the program and app should plug directly into it.

- **Buddy dyads (the standout retention + transfer mechanism).** Pair every learner with a weekly 30–60 min 1:1 partner for the full 16 weeks. This *operationalizes* three requirements already named: AGT distributed witnessing (§Part 6, no sole mirror), Distributed Redundancy across contexts, and the ecology's need for a reinforcing relationship. Ships with a hardcoded question bank (parts found this week / push-pull dynamics / Self-access moments / Hero's-Journey stage) and four comms principles: **confidentiality, non-judgment, equality (no hierarchy), self-awareness over advice-giving.** Cheap, high-adherence, and it re-embeds change in a real human relationship — the exact antidote to the frictionless-AI-companion failure mode. `[H]`
- **Soft Gaze as the daily anchor practice** (app §3F.1): notice parts → thank → ask to step back → rest in Self. The regulation/unblending ritual that opens every session and every day. `[C]`
- **8-quality Self Radar** (app §1.15) replaces a single "how Self-led are you" gut check with a measurable wheel; the learner develops the weakest quality one at a time with a targeted micro-practice. Assessed at intake, re-assessed at Graduation as a visible arc. `[C]`
- **Exact part-dialogue script** (Who are you? / positive intention? / what do you want for me? / how do you do it? → thank), and the **polarization resolution** move — find the two parts' *shared* deeper goal, never pick a winner (app §3F.2). Sharpens the Block 3.x reconsolidation work. `[C]`
- **Boundary:** shadow-via-projection and Hero's-Journey framing are `[C]` narrative scaffolds, held lightly — used to *surface* material for Self-led inquiry, never as a typology verdict.

## PART 9D — ARC CORRECTIONS (from the developmental synthesis)

Three structural corrections the deep-developmental literature says *every* linear change program gets wrong. Cheap to add, high-leverage.

- **Holonic Respiration — growth is a breath, not a ladder.** Every forward differentiation (a hard session, an unblending, a boundary) needs a **return/refuel beat**, not as regression but as the metabolic mechanism of consolidation (Mahler's rapprochement; the hero's return). This *sharpens* the Dual-Phase rhythm (§Part 3): the Resonance session is not "rest between challenges" — it is the in-breath that makes the out-breath survivable. Never shame the oscillation back toward safety; design it. Forcing linear-forward progress produces brittle, dissociated gains that collapse under stress. `[C]`
- **Grief as the separation tax.** Every integration is a loss — of the protector's old job, of the self that organized around the wound. Add an explicit **grief beat** at each integration (the Funeral-for-the-Protector ritual is exactly this): thank and mourn the part's retiring role. Grief (natural response to necessary loss) ≠ trauma processing (overwhelming event); they need different moves. Un-grieved separation = incomplete integration — the part quietly re-fuses. `[C]`
- **Two engines: heal *from* AND live *toward*.** The program is deficit-framed. Add the attraction engine at every phase, not just the end: cultivate what the learner *loves*, finds beautiful, is summoned by (Plotkin's soul-encounter; Cooperrider's Appreciative Inquiry — systems grow toward what they persistently ask about). The Future Self (§Block 4.2) is reframed from "less wounded" to "more devoted to something." A program with only the first engine produces sterile, dissociated detachment; only the second, spiritual bypass. Both, or neither. `[C]`
