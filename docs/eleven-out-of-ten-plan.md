## 1. The experience to aim for

**Help me notice something specific, question it, try one useful move, and carry forward what I choose to keep. Make that feel like exploring a small, beautifully authored world.**

The reference bar is The Pattern for personal-insight mechanics and Duolingo for clarity, play, and presentation. Innerverse should earn its own identity through a tactile Felt cosmos, explainable observations, user-authored experiments, and continuity that respects the person's corrections.

An exceptional first encounter has a complete arc:

1. **Arrive:** the preserved authored loading scene yields as soon as the requested experience is ready.
2. **Recognize:** start with an ordinary situation or open an existing tool; assessment is an available route.
3. **Investigate:** consider a small number of plausible readings, answer a useful distinguishing question, or supply different wording.
4. **Choose:** leave with a sentence, environmental adjustment, practice, or human-support action.
5. **Keep:** explicitly save a useful pocket tool, with its source and an easy way to revise it.
6. **Return:** compare what happened with what was expected, keeping exceptions and adaptations visible.

“11/10” is the ambition for this connected experience. Passing the existing rubrics is the foundation. Exceeding expectations means people can identify a specific benefit, voluntarily prefer the experience to a simpler alternative, and use something from it outside the app. It is not an additional numeric score to assign ourselves.

## 3. The signature mechanics

These are concrete design directions to prototype and judge, not claims that people already find them useful.

### A. A discovery table that changes when I correct it

Show an observation, two plausible readings, and one question that distinguishes them. Let “neither,” “depends,” an exception, or the person's own wording materially change the visible working formulation. Keep private free text verbatim unless the person explicitly requests an interpretation. Never resurrect a rejected reading as a fresh discovery.

The payoff is a source-linked sentence the user recognizes and can correct, followed by one relevant action. Avoid generic reassurance and long report screens before the person has received a useful result.

**Beads:** `.2.31`, `.2.6`, `.2.11`, `.2.13`, `.2.27`, `.2.28`, `.2.29` under `ArchetypeAI-28h`.

**Judge it:** can a person explain what was observed, what is tentative, what changed after their correction, and what they might do next?

### B. A thread of evidence, including the exception

Turn the existing recent-choice trace and personal comparison prototypes into one coherent interaction. The person selects moments that may belong together, compares exact sources, and can add an exception or say they do not belong together. A thread has a user-written title and an editable working question; the app does not infer a stable psychological trait from repeated button choices.

Use a small paper thread or constellation connection as the visual expression. Show the same sources in an ordinary list. Keep present state, recurring evidence, personal authorship, and canonical assessment scores separate.

**Beads:** `.2.31`, `.3.3`, `.5.1`, `.5.6`.

**Judge it:** can a person trace every statement to its source, find the exception, and revise/delete the thread without changing source answers or scores?

### C. A useful move that becomes a pocket tool

Connect an insight or chosen intention to a reviewed practice, an editable sentence, or an environmental change. The saved card contains the cue, chosen move, practical limit, fallback/resource, and optional revisit. Adaptation is a useful outcome; “did not try,” “did not help,” and “do not record” remain complete paths.

Give the card one optional, coherent physical expression in the Felt world. It remains readable, editable, and exportable without the scene. At a later visit, bring back the chosen tool and its context with the person's permission, rather than making them start over.

**Beads:** `.3.1`, `.3.2`, `.3.3`, `.3.4`, `.3.5`, `.5.4`.

**Judge it:** can a person use the tool away from the app, return to the correct version, and report what changed without being pushed toward a positive outcome?

### D. One rehearsal worth playing

Elevate the existing ordinary-request Replay into a signature scene. Watch provides a brief, stoppable setup. Director lets the person change a practical constraint or try a different sentence; the imagined interaction visibly responds. The result is a chosen move that can enter the existing experiment card.

Use tactile staging and gentle visual comedy around everyday friction. Keep the user's concern treated with care. Every useful choice and output is also available through guided text. Expand the scene catalogue after comparison shows where interaction helps.

**Beads:** `.4.1`, `.4.2`, `.4.3`, `.4.4`, `.7.1`.

**Judge it:** does Replay improve comprehension, preparation, or willingness to try the move compared with concise guided text? Longer playtime alone is insufficient.

### E. A cosmos that is playful and analytically exact

Give ordinary exploration a clear selected-character response, relationship explanation, source panel, and useful next action. Preserve Felt identity and materials across arrival, report, map, coach, and booking.

In the therapist observatory, maintain the complete 3+1 workspace: distinct axes, fourth-axis slice, linked table, full-vector pair comparison, and source-specific overlays. Preserve the complete 18-character 147-derived cast and full 64-derived pantheon where authorized. Show the provenance and meaning of reference, derived, measured, user-positioned, and unknown coordinates. Display layout changes must not alter scientific values.

Unknown positions remain available through the list. Relationship hypotheses, personal scores, and historical therapist notes remain separately labeled. The authorized client route must never briefly show the therapist's personal data.

**Beads:** `.2.7`, `.2.8`, `.2.9`, `.2.20`, `.2.21`, `.2.22`, `.2.23`, `.2.24`, `.2.29`, `.2.30`.

**Judge it:** can a user explain a selected character, and can an authorized therapist reproduce an exact comparison from the table and full coordinates without mistaking reference placement for measurement?

### F. Delight that describes something real

Author a compact catalogue around real events: the ready arrival, a saved paper bookmark, a selected-part reaction, a quiet coach guide, and a ticket whose state accurately reflects requested or confirmed booking. Give each a clear trigger, coherent material, interruption behavior, static equivalent, and localization.

Provide immediately visible control feedback; optional motion can continue around it. Sound, motion, density, and ambient activity remain independently controllable. Quiet mode retains the same essential actions and outcomes. Scored assessment answers receive emotionally neutral feedback so play does not reward a score direction.

Optional collectibles represent specific acts of authorship or practice. They never certify psychological mastery, create missed-day debt, or gate support.

**Beads:** `.2.1`, `.2.2`, `.2.10`, `.3.4`, `.5.3`, `.5.4`, `.7.2`, `.7.4`.

**Judge it:** can people predict what an interaction will do, enjoy or hide the flourish, and finish just as comfortably with motion/sound disabled?

### G. Continuity that saves me from retelling

Start from the existing private shelf, comparisons, postcards, and inspectable memory. Add a deliberately selected evidence import before attempting broader synthesis. Every accepted observation keeps its excerpt/location, date, authorship, status, and retention rule. Present contradictions and correction controls alongside any synthesis.

For coach use, preview the exact selected context and destination. User-directed personal part edits receive a saved result, provenance, version history, and undo. AI tools cannot modify canonical 64/147 answers or scores, therapist-private notes, or invent map coordinates. Rejected extractions and deleted sources must not survive as ambient context.

**Beads:** `.2.11`, `.2.12`, `.2.13`, `.5.1`, `.5.6`, `.5.7`.

**Judge it:** can people reuse what they already provided, trace and correct an extraction, inspect an AI edit, and remove the original and its derived records under the declared retention contract?

## 7. Proposed measures for the next evaluation

These are initial design targets to validate and refine. They are not measured results, statistical guarantees, or claimed clinical outcomes.

- **First useful result:** offer a complete short path around two minutes, with visible effort and a comfortable exit. Observe actual time and quality; revise if the speed target encourages shallow interpretation or pressure.
- **Unprompted comprehension:** start with a formative round of 5–8 people with varied language, sensory, and access preferences. Observe finding the route, correcting a reading, saving/resuming a tool, and controlling sharing. Seek independent completion of each critical task; any recurring misunderstanding triggers revision. Report raw observations rather than a population success percentage from a small sample.
- **Return and transfer:** invite consenting volunteers to try a chosen move and revisit over roughly 1–2 weeks. Record what was used, adapted, unhelpful, forgotten, or deliberately left behind. Include departures and declined reminders in the account of results.
- **Mobile responsiveness:** initially target LCP ≤2.5 s, CLS ≤0.1, and INP ≤200 ms at the 75th percentile when an adequate real-user sample exists. Use explicit lab/device traces beforehand; lab load measurements do not establish field INP or percentiles.
- **Scene economy:** aim for stable 60 fps on a declared mainstream device; provide a comfortable static/list alternative on less capable hardware. Measure repeated entries and exits for increasing memory, renderer count, long tasks, and active loops. Declare the device/network/content scope before interpreting results.
- **Access:** zero critical or serious automated findings in reviewed states, plus manual checks of all essential actions, screen-reader speech, focus order, text resizing, and contrast. Automated absence of findings is only one part of acceptance.
- **Live services:** exercise authorized success and denial, cancellation, late/duplicate responses, competing booking requests, timezone boundaries, and consent expiry. Record actual provider/account evidence with sensitive content excluded.
- **Reference comparisons:** use actual obtainable Pattern and Duolingo experiences at matching task and viewport. If only public material is available, restrict judgments to what it shows. Compare insight to insight and feedback to feedback; a marketing homepage does not establish superiority over a signed-in product journey.

## 9. The continuing Gauntlet

For every meaningful slice:

1. Read its Bead and linked contract. State the user task and concrete comparison material.
2. Give a builder the smallest complete change and a separate critic fresh context. The critic inspects the actual output rather than effort, plans, or screenshots alone.
3. Compare comparable tasks at matching viewports. Hide identifying labels where feasible and note where recognizable products prevent true blinding. Use a plain/static version when it is the relevant simpler alternative.
4. Ask the critic which better serves this task and what single gap matters most. Keep scores subordinate to observed reasons.
5. Implement that gap, verify the changed boundaries and connected journeys, and inspect the resulting exact build.
6. Update Beads, [the progress page](gauntlet-progress.md), and [the evidence register](content-evidence-register.md). Do not transfer a previous build's score to a new one.
7. Continue until the slice meets its applicable criteria and wins its relevant comparison; move to the next slice and revisit earlier ones when integration exposes a regression. Keep unavailable device, participant, clinical, hardware and service evidence explicitly pending while continuing other authorized work.

After a slice passes, seek the next meaningful improvement in usefulness, comprehension, comfort, or transfer. Choose it from observations and user feedback. Cosmetic variation alone does not demonstrate an elevation.

The lasting success is a coherent world that helps people understand, act, remember, and become less dependent on the app—while remaining pleasant to return to by choice.
