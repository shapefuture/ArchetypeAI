# Innerverse — v3 "Plush Cosmos" (characters from the references, cosmos as the stage)

Supersedes the cosmic-ontology plans (`innerverse-character-redesign-plan.md` v2, `innerverse-visualisation-plan.md`, `innerverse-image-prompts.md`). Those are rejected.

## Why v2 failed (the honest version)
1. **I removed what makes the references lovable.** The references work because of a **soft toy body, big readable eyes, a saturated single colour and one cute prop.** v2 replaced all of that with darkness, gas and rock.
2. **I confused "cosmic" with "astrophysical".** The user wanted the *world* to be a universe, not the *characters* to be celestial phenomena.
3. **I confused "SIGGRAPH-tier" with "photoreal physics".** Award-winning character work is about **appeal**: shape, charm, readability and craft. Physics is in service of that.
4. **People bond with faces and softness,** not with metaphors that need explaining. If a character only makes sense after reading the plan, it has already failed.
5. **I designed in text and never sketched.** Any sketch would have exposed v2 in seconds. From now on, every proposal gets a 2D lineup before anything else.

## The v3 rule
**The characters are plush toys. The cosmos is their home.** Cosmic identity lives in the *world* and in *one small celestial prop per character*, never in replacing the character.

## 1. Character rules (taken directly from the references)
| Rule | From reference | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **One chunky silhouette** | Refs 2, 3 | Blob, cloud, frog-lump, triangle, heart, bean, drop, crescent, rounded square. It must read as a flat colour fill at 32px. |
| **Flocked felt / velvet** | Refs 2, 3, 4 | A single saturated colour with a soft fuzz rim. That fuzz rim *is* the 2D outline (light catches it). |
| **Face = eyes first** | Refs 3, 4 | Glossy black bead eyes or white-and-black googly eyes. Lids show mood (half-closed, wide, sideways). A tiny optional mouth line. |
| **No arms, no legs** | Ref 3 | Expression comes from squash, lean, tilt, bounce and eyes. |
| **Exactly one prop** | Ref 3 (beret, bowtie, glasses) | Made **cosmic and tiny** (see §3). |
| **Colour does the casting** | Refs 3, 4 | Each part gets its own vivid hue. Nothing is mid-grey or muddy. |

## 2. Role families (silhouette language, readable without a legend)
- **Self:** a warm round sun-plush with soft felt petal rays (like the red flower in ref 2), gentle inner glow and calm closed-smile eyes. It's the only one that glows.
- **Managers:** stable, grounded geometric shapes (rounded square, triangle, pentagon, tall bean). Their props are "structure": a ring, a monocle, a little hat.
- **Firefighters:** spiky or lobed energetic shapes (flower, flame-lump, star-blob), the hottest saturated colours, with props that move (a comet-tail tuft, a sparkler star).
- **Exiles:** small, soft, rounded shapes (drop, crescent, cloud, mochi) in pastel colours, with sleepy or teary eyes. Each is tucked under a felt-cloud blanket or a moon hood.

## 3. Cosmic prop library (one per character, all interchangeable)
Saturn-ring hoop · tiny orbiting felt moon · star sprout (instead of the leaf in ref 4) · comet-tail tuft · crescent-moon nightcap · constellation freckles (stitched dots) · little telescope monocle · aurora scarf · meteor beret · planet-bobble antenna · sunspot blush · star bowtie.

## 4. The world (where the cosmos lives)
- **A cozy toy universe,** not a void: a deep indigo-to-violet gradient sky with soft felt clouds, hand-stitched stars and a few plush planets in the background.
- **The map is a floating orrery diorama.** The characters sit on **little felt moon-islands and floating pebble platforms** at different heights and depths (ref 1's stepped platforms, in space). Platform position encodes the axes: AGENCY left–right, TEMPERATURE height, FOCUS depth.
- **Attachment** is shown by a thin brass orbit wire to the Self, or by a felt yarn thread: short and tight = anxious, long and loose = avoidant.
- **Relationships** are drawn as glowing yarn threads between characters.
- **Lighting:** a warm key light from the Self-sun and a cool rim light from the sky, with soft contact shadows on the platforms. It's toy-photography lighting, with a slight tilt-shift.

## 5. Planet/Star idea kept as a cute healing state (not a replacement)
An unburdened part **starts to glow from inside** (warm light through the felt) and earns a tiny star on its prop. The constellation lights up as the user heals. It's the same idea as before, but now it happens to a character people care about.

## 6. Customization
Silhouette (12) × colour (any) × eyes (8 styles × 6 lid moods) × optional mouth (5) × cosmic prop (12) × size × glow. Every combination stays cute because the material, lighting and proportions are fixed by the system.

## 7. Validation before any 3D
1. **2D lineup sheet:** 12 characters as flat vector fills plus eyes on white. Can you tell them apart and do you like them?
2. **The same 12 characters as 3D plush renders** (image generator), to check that 2D and 3D match.
3. **"Which one is you?" test:** 5 people pick the character they identify with and say why. Emotional pull is the success metric.

## 10. Review of first generations + three creature styles
Generated: playmat (flat felt), sky islands (velvet, Pixar light), white lineup (flocked velvet).
- **Works:** flocked velvet material (lineup), eyelid-driven expression (sky islands: sleepy square, stern triangle), flat felt readability (playmat), embroidered constellation on the crescent, the petal sun.
- **Fails:** every face uses the same formula (two eyes, smile, blush), so personality reads as generic AI kawaii. Sphere-ish shapes (peach, teal ball) have weak silhouettes. The frog grew legs. Props are random (rocket, magnifier) with no shared material language. Every character is the same size, and the rainbow colour order gives no hierarchy.

Three creature styles (each with an ownable signature):
1. **Velvet Bean:** smooth flocked velvet, big bead eyes set low and wide, no mouth, appliqué felt eyelids carry the mood, gold-thread cosmic embroidery.
2. **Stitch Appliqué:** flat pillow felt, contrast blanket-stitch outline used as the line art, embroidered eyes, everything readable as a 2D icon.
3. **Fluff & Gloss:** long-pile fluffy fur (like the original references), tiny black bead eyes, every prop in glossy black (beret, bowtie, glasses DNA) translated to cosmic objects.
Prompts are in the session reply.
