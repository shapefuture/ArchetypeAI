## Design contradictions resolved in the plan

- **Novelty versus predictability:** explicit surprise modes inside stable navigation, with previewable formats and quiet equivalents.
- **Freedom versus care:** expressive facets and pseudonyms with recipient authority, clear boundaries, accountable moderation and optional repair.
- **Collective magic versus consent:** world changes come from approved real artifacts; cross-listing, remix, audience expansion and archival have separate permission decisions.
- **Recognition versus comparison:** contextual contribution stories and optional personal progress; no global person rank, basic-access lock or penalty for rest.
- **Generosity versus exploitation:** gifts have no debt; effort, pay, scope, ownership and cancellation are visible; care workload and compensation are measured.
- **Beautiful richness versus overload/performance:** licensed modular scenes with independent sensory controls and equivalent calm text functionality.
- **AI amplification versus human authorship:** default-off bounded assistance, explicit input preview, labeling and human publish decisions; no generated friends or identity voices.
- **Ambition versus network cold start:** concentrated real hosted windows and solo value before broad expansion; no fake social activity.

## Carried-forward boundaries

Public matching uses chosen fields and per-purpose consent. Private assessment, journal, therapist and authored parts content remain private unless the person explicitly reviews a permitted share. Plural-informed accommodations concern user-authored terminology and continuity, not inference of identities or a game persona treated as diagnosis. Public creativity and ordinary peer support are distinct from clinical or spiritual services and efficacy claims.

Firebase Spark is approved. Billing-dependent Cloud Storage was skipped; text-first artifacts, licensed local fixtures and existing permitted references enable development while media uploads remain deferred. Do not enable billing or live payments. Optional economic models are reviewable prototypes, not commercial commitments. Do not send invitations or recruit through external messaging without actual authorization.

The existing phone statement remains exactly **“tested and fine.”** It is not a measured report on new world views. User research, accessibility sessions, real phone traces and benefit follow-up must be genuinely obtained with consent. A plan, mock, agent review, imported task or closed planning-control Bead is not feature implementation or participant evidence.
