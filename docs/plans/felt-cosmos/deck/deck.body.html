The idea that joins both styles

From sketch to stitch

A doodle is a pattern; felt is the sewn thing. Common parts, examples, feelings and the sample map are ink sketches on paper. Motion

Every motion reports a truth

Physics over curves, transform and opacity only, nothing over a second blocks input. Springs ship as CSS linear() curves; GSAP (already a dependency) choreographs; the View Transitions API carries a part from shelf to page. Reduced motion always wins.
