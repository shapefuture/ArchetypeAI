Intersecting axes of **Stance** (Yielding/Passive ↔ Asserting/Active) and **Locus** (Internal/Self ↔ External/World) produce the four irreducible exchange verbs:

| | Internal (Self-Referenced) | External (World-Referenced) |

| **Asserting (Active)** | **TAKE** — claim, decide, initiate, extract resources for self-direction | **GIVE** — offer care, value, connection, co-create with others |

These are the machine code. Every behavior, every thought, every relationship can be mapped to one or a sequence of these verbs.

### III. THE SEVEN META-CAPACITIES: MEASURING THE SYSTEM’S THROUGHPUT

Talent is not a static profile but the **dynamic capacity to metabolize free energy into realization**. This is quantified by seven measurable dimensions, each a gauge on the diagnostic panel:

1. **Range** — the total accessible territory across all four verbs, eight sub-phases, and three tonal registers.
2. **Velocity** — the speed and fluidity of switching between the appropriate verb/tonality for context.
3. **Coherence** — the ability to hold opposing energies (e.g., Edge Take and Core Give) simultaneously without fragmentation.
4. **Calibration** — the accuracy of matching verb, tone, and sub-phase to the contextual domain and panarchy phase.
5. **Zero-Point Recovery Rate** — the time required to return to the still, sovereign centre after an activation cycle.
6. **Fidelity** — the degree of existential alignment between current operations and the system’s deepest purpose/values (Ori, Ma’at).
7. **Eros Efficiency (η)** — the proportion of total free energy intake that is converted into **lasting transformation, creative output, deepened love, and collective realization**, versus what is lost to entropy (shadow maintenance, repetition compulsion, burnout).

The ultimate index of a system’s health is its **Eros Efficiency multiplied by Fidelity** — because even highly efficient conversion toward a false purpose is hollow.
